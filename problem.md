# Problem

Home cooks planning a party or cooking for a large group need a recipe for a different number of people than what the original recipe was written for. Trying to convert the ingredients manually can take lots of time and could be inaccurate.

# Initial requirements

- R1: Enter a recipe with ingredients, quantities, units, and servings
- R2: Scale the recipe to a new number of people being served and show the adjustments
- R3: Keep a pantry list and alert the user if ingredients aren't available in the list
- R4: Save the recipes and pantry data
